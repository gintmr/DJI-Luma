Quick answers

Questions

What is in the reproduction kit?

Screen behavior and states, the technical stack, Bluetooth and Wi-Fi flows, the scheduler, ADB steps, staged prompts, 19 troubleshooting cases, an acceptance checklist and 18 light/dark screen diagrams.

Which connections are needed?

During development, a computer debugs the Android phone over USB. In daily use, the phone controls the camera over Bluetooth and uses Wi-Fi for live view and the camera album.

How do I start building?

Download the kit, add your phone, camera and computer details, then give the brief and references to your Agent. Work through setup and UI, Bluetooth capture, scheduling, Wi-Fi features, then polish and regression checks.
